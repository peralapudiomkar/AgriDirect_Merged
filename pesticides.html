Always read and follow the instructions on the product label.

Keep agricultural chemicals in their original labelled containers and away from children.

🧑‍🌾 Smart Farming Advice

Before using any crop-protection product

1. Identify the crop and problem correctly.
2. Monitor the field and understand the level of damage.
3. Prefer preventive and integrated approaches where appropriate.
4. Consult an authorised agricultural professional when uncertain.
5. If a product is recommended, read its current label carefully and follow all safety instructions.
